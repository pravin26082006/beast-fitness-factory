CUSTOMIZED PATHWAYS

OUR FITNESS PROGRAMS

Structured training programs designed for visible results and athletic durability.

POWER & IRON

1. Strength Training

Master compound barbell movements (squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press) with systematic progressive overload to maximize raw power.

HYPERTROPHY

2. Muscle Building

Targeted hypertrophy protocols focusing on mechanical tension, metabolic stress, and muscle balance to build a dense, aesthetic physique.

FAT LOSS & TONING

3. Weight Management

Sustainable fat loss programs combining resistance training with metabolic conditioning to preserve lean muscle while burning excess body fat.

1-ON-1 COACHING

4. Personal Training

Dedicated one-on-one sessions with Head Coach Gowtham or Coach Srinath, customized to your biomechanics, schedule, and specific targets.

ENDURANCE

5. Cardio Training

Enhance cardiovascular capacity, VO2 max, and stamina using high-intensity interval training (HIIT), rowers, assault bikes, and treadmills.

MOBILITY & AGILITY

6. Functional Fitness

Kettlebells, battle ropes, plyometrics, and mobility work designed to build real-world strength, joint durability, and injury resilience.
